Menu music

Dynamic Audio plays it — a song from its library (data/<user>/assets/bgm), at its music volume — only while this menu is open. When you close the menu, Dynamic Audio goes back to whatever it was doing (or silence).

Worldlines

Lists every D-Mail this chat kept (including ones from older versions), lets you put them on the map or take them off, and can estimate the readings they never recorded. Send a D-Mail that stops an earlier one (from any phone) and that change is undone; the meter returns to where it was before it. After a D-Mail, recent mail is rewritten to match the new past (one extra generation). You keep what you remember: ◇ on a mail shows the old version; mail that never existed hides under the inbox. Undoing the D-Mail puts it all back.

Card history

Every time Apply rewrites a character card, the text it replaced is kept here so you can undo it (or go back to the original). Stored only in this SillyTavern's extension settings — never inside the card file, so exporting or sharing a card doesn't carry it. Undoing a D-Mail also offers to restore the card it changed. /cardundo [name]

Convergence

Outcomes the worldline keeps reaching however anyone tries to stop them — until the divergence crosses into another attractor field (the reading's first digit changes). Told to the AI with the worldline. After each time leap, a short check asks whether the abandoned future reached them.

Milestones

Crossing an attractor field makes the meter's first tube hesitate and adds one quiet line for the AI. Reaching your target reading gives the home meter a faint glow.

Memories sent back through PhoneWave.

Call PhoneWave from the phone (or /timeleap) to open TLM.EXE: pick who leaps (you or a character), the message to arrive at, and the story times. The chat is branched at that message; the leaper keeps compressed memories of the abandoned future.
Your memories are always injected; a character's only while that character speaks.

Characters: D-Mail & Time Leap

Characters can send D-Mails and time leap too: from their phone (grab it), when you make them, or on their own. When a character's D-Mail shifts the worldline only you notice (Reading Steiner); the sender does not remember sending it. Every few character messages, a short check asks whether someone has a real reason — they know the technology exists, and there is something urgent to prevent or undo. Usually the answer is no. When a message shows a death, an attack or a disaster, the check runs right away — no waiting for the message count or cooldown — and if they decide to leap, they leap without asking you first.
Off: their leap just happens (the chat branches).
Also /chardmail Name guidance and /charleap Name guidance. Forced leaps don't ask. Runs the "would they?" check for the character above right now (no cooldown). It is still their call — usually no. Also /worldcheck [Name].
